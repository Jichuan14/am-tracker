AM Track beta for Apple Silicon Macs
Requires macOS 13 or later and the macOS Music app.

INSTALL
1. Unzip the download and move AM Track.app to Applications.
2. Open AM Track. This beta is not signed with an Apple Developer ID or
   notarized by Apple, so macOS may block its first launch.
3. If you trust this download, open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   find the blocked-app message for AM Track, and choose Open Anyway.
   Follow the confirmation prompts, then open the app again if needed.

Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/102445

FIRST LAUNCH
Open Music, then choose Enable recording in AM Track's setup screen.
Allow AM Track to control Music when macOS asks. If access was denied,
use Open Automation settings in AM Track, then enable Music access under
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Automation.

Enable recording starts collecting your own library metadata and future
listening on this Mac. Not now leaves Music access and recording off.
Play a song in Music to begin building history. Recommendations may need
library metadata, play counts, or more listening before anything appears.
Music's library must contain saved songs for library recommendations.

EVERYDAY USE
The menu bar shows On or Off. Start/Stop recording controls new history
entries. Library details and playback controls can still refresh after
you stop recording, once you have enabled Music access.
Closing the window keeps the app running. Quit stops it. Your recording
choice is remembered when you reopen AM Track. No login item is installed.
History.dat imports are optional and run only when explicitly requested.

YOUR DATA
New installations store data in:
~/Library/Application Support/AM Track/
Use Open data folder to see the active location. An existing database
beside the app, or a previously selected data folder, may be used instead.
History, library metadata, ratings, backups, and bounded queue diagnostics
stay local. AM Track does not upload them or send analytics.
The Music app itself may contact Apple's services for playback.
Export history creates a CSV that can contain private listening data.
Keep that file and the data folder private. Quit AM Track before backing
up its data. Updating the app does not replace the data folder.

LIMITATIONS
This download supports Apple Silicon only; Intel Macs are not supported.
Recording requires AM Track to be running while the Mac is awake and
observes Music playback on this Mac. It does not collect iPhone activity.
Queue additions rely on a private macOS interface and verified catalog
IDs; availability can vary by macOS version and track. A queue error does
not mean recording has stopped. You can continue playback in Music.
